# Credits

Third-party assets in ORQWA, with their sources and licences (R47, ADR 0009). Only own work,
CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA are allowed.

The app's settings show these credits and every licence notice of the software built into it
on their About tab (R83, ADR 0018): ORQWA's GPL, the Godot engine and its components, FFmpeg's
libraries (LGPL 2.1 or later) and every Rust crate, from `core/orqwa-app/notices/`.

## Models

The East Asian house and castle archetypes for Asian rides, Nordic painted-timber houses and
stone keeps, and French village houses and château-inspired keeps (R164) are own work,
scripted in `art/buildings/japanese.py`, `art/buildings/nordic.py` and
`art/buildings/french.py` (GPL-3.0), reusing the project's existing materials and textures.
Architectural references are listed in [regional buildings](regional-buildings.md); no
reference imagery or third-party models are bundled.

The fractured boulders and shared cliff/boulder weathering shader (R115) are own work
(GPL-3.0). The rock albedo and OpenGL normal are converted from
[Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry) by **Dimitrios Savva**
(photography) and **Rico Cilliers** (processing), Poly Haven, **CC0 1.0**. Untouched
1K originals and their source are kept in `art/sources/rock-boulder-dry/`;
`art/textures/rock_scan.py` converts them to the shared rock WebP maps.

Bridge concrete (R122) uses [Concrete Wall 007](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_007)
by **Charlotte Baglioni** (photography), **Dario Barresi** (processing) and **Rico Cilliers**
(tiling), Poly Haven, **CC0 1.0**. Untouched 1K albedo, OpenGL normal and roughness maps,
source URLs and checksums are kept in `art/sources/concrete-wall-007/`.
`art/textures/concrete_scan.py` converts them to dedicated bridge WebP maps. The app
bundles these assets and downloads nothing for them at runtime.

Urban concrete and paving textures are own work, baked from the procedural materials in
`art/textures/build.py` (GPL-3.0); sidewalks, curbs and building surroundings are generated
by `core/orqwa-world/src/urban.rs`.

City benches and bins reuse the own-work models generated by `art/props/build.py` (GPL-3.0).

Road hierarchy and surface rendering (R119) reuse these own-work asphalt, gravel, dirt,
grass, concrete and paving textures. Road tags come from the OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap
tiles already bundled in courses; no additional data source or asset licence is introduced.

Building and vegetation models, including the authored tree LODs from
`art/vegetation/lods.py`, and the other textures (`app/assets/models/buildings/`,
`app/assets/models/vegetation/`, `app/assets/textures/`) are own work, generated by the
Blender scripts in `art/` (GPL-3.0 like the rest of ORQWA); see
[art/README.md](../art/README.md).

The spreading and upright fern models are also own work, generated by
`art/vegetation/build.py`; their leaflets are geometry using the shared frond material.

The riders' bodies (`app/assets/models/riders/`) are derived from the realistic bodies and
skeleton of [Human Base Meshes](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/) v1.4.1 by
**Blender Studio** and contributors, **CC0 1.0**; the extract is kept in
`art/sources/human-base-meshes/` (see its `SOURCE.md`). Their kit, helmets and bikes are own
work, generated by `art/riders/build.py`.

## Device protocol references

The Tacx Bluetooth codec is original code based on Tacx's
[How-to FE-C over BLE v1.0.0](https://github.com/jedla22/BleTrainerControl/blob/master/How-to%20FE-C%20over%20BLE%20v1_0_0.pdf).
FE-C field layouts were cross-checked against
[GoldenCheetah](https://github.com/GoldenCheetah/GoldenCheetah/blob/master/src/ANT/ANTMessage.cpp)
and [FortiusANT](https://github.com/WouterJD/FortiusANT/blob/master/pythoncode/antDongle.py).
No implementation code was copied.

## Riding audio

All riding recordings are **CC0 1.0** (public domain), verified on their original Freesound
pages. Bundled excerpts are mixed to mono, filtered, resampled to 24 kHz and level adjusted:

- `core/orqwa-app/audio/wind.pcm`: **ecfike**, [Wind and Leaves.wav](https://freesound.org/people/ecfike/sounds/183496/), seconds 12–48 of the HQ preview.
- `core/orqwa-app/audio/rain.pcm`: **soundrecorder7**, [Light Rain](https://freesound.org/people/soundrecorder7/sounds/167034/), seconds 2–22 of the HQ preview. CC0 licence checked on the source page on 2026-10-27.
- `core/orqwa-app/audio/chain.pcm`: **dawidwmika**, [bicycle forward movement](https://freesound.org/people/dawidwmika/sounds/380288/), seconds 8–24 of the HQ preview.
- `core/orqwa-app/audio/freewheel.pcm`: **yurkobb**, [bike-wheel-free-looped.flac](https://freesound.org/people/yurkobb/sounds/274481/), first 2 seconds of the HQ preview.
- `core/orqwa-app/audio/shift.pcm`: **ChemiCatz**, [bike_gear_shift.wav](https://freesound.org/people/ChemiCatz/sounds/267910/), seconds 0.08–0.38 of the HQ preview, one mechanical gear change.
- `core/orqwa-app/audio/shift-chain-{1,2,3}.pcm`: **stib**, [changingGears.wav](https://freesound.org/people/stib/sounds/494322/), seconds 6.65–7.75, 8.05–9.15 and 10.65–11.75 of the HQ preview. Close-miked chain transfers between front rings, retaining the rattle and settling after engagement.

[Processing and reproduction](../core/orqwa-app/audio/README.md) use the existing Rust audio
decoder. The live wind model and texture mixer are ORQWA's own work (GPL-3.0). These sources
are bundled in the app; riders do not contact Freesound or its CDN.

## Data

The offline Asian and Nordic architecture classifiers (`core/orqwa-world/data/asia.json` and
`nordic.json`) retain their unchanged country polygons from the same pinned, ISC-licensed
country-coder source used below. The French classifier uses a small hand-drawn outline of
metropolitan France and Corsica (`france.json`). Their provenance and the bundled licence are linked in
[the data README](../core/orqwa-world/data/README.md).

Country/territory traffic-side defaults are factual reference data cross-checked against
[World Standards' driving-side list](https://www.worldstandards.eu/cars/list-of-left-driving-countries/).
No text, map artwork or implementation was copied.

The small offline outlines for Hong Kong, Macau and the US Virgin Islands
(`core/orqwa-osm/data/traffic-territories.json`) come from
[`country-coder`](https://github.com/rapideditor/country-coder/blob/c30d4314809cb8a446c3d42181cd370a1a424056/src/data/borders.json),
copyright 2026 country-coder contributors, **ISC**. The original licence accompanies the
data; no upstream implementation code was copied.

The London cycleway review fixture (`core/fixtures/london-cycleway.gpx`) is extracted from
the prepared OpenFreeMap transportation data, © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0).

Map and terrain data credits are shown in the app and stored in every course file; see
[Courses](https://github.com/ORQWA/ORQWA/wiki/Courses).

Map detail (R142, [ADR 0033](adr/0033-map-detail.md)) reads the regions riders download from
[Geofabrik's download server](https://download.geofabrik.de), which offers them free of
charge: OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0). The road surfaces and
roundabouts ORQWA keeps of them, and carries in course files, are shared under the same
licence. The test download `core/orqwa-osm/tests/data/bern.osm.pbf` is made by its tests.

The sample course ORQWA ships (`core/orqwa-gd/courses/twann-chasseral.tqc`, R95) holds such
data: map tiles © OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles, data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0), and
terrain by Mapterhorn (CC BY 4.0) and AWS Terrain Tiles.

The list of ISO 3166-1 country codes that matches the map's three-letter codes to two-letter
ones (`core/orqwa-osm/src/iso.rs`) is taken from Debian's
[iso-codes](https://salsa.debian.org/iso-codes-team/iso-codes) (`data/iso_3166-1.json`,
LGPL 2.1 or later). The test tiles in `core/fixtures/countries/` are cut from OpenFreeMap
tiles: © OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles, data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0).
