# Credits

Third-party assets in Torqa, with their sources and licences (R47, ADR 0009). Only own work,
CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA are allowed.

## Models

Building, vegetation and cloud models (`app/assets/models/buildings/`,
`app/assets/models/vegetation/`, `app/assets/models/clouds/`) are own work, generated by the
Blender scripts in `art/` (GPL-3.0 like the rest of Torqa); see
[art/README.md](../art/README.md).

The riders' bodies (`app/assets/models/riders/`) are derived from the stylized "primitive"
bodies of [Human Base Meshes](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/) v1.4.1 by
**Blender Studio** and contributors, **CC0 1.0**; the extract is kept in
`art/sources/human-base-meshes/` (see its `SOURCE.md`). Their kit, helmets and bikes are own
work, generated by `art/riders/build.py`.

The Blender skills in `.claude/skills/blender-*` are from
