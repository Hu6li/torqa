# Start page

Torqa opens on the start page with four tabs:

- **Courses** — your course library as cards: each shows the route in blue on black with its
  elevation underneath, the distance, climbing and steepest gradient. **Import GPX or
  course…** prepares a GPX route (terrain, map, 3D world) and adds it to the library, or adds
  a `.tqc` course file ([courses.md](courses.md)). Click a card for the course page: route,
  elevation profile with its climbs, your best times there, the ride options (camera,
  difficulty, descents, time of day, weather), who to race ([ghosts.md](ghosts.md)) and
  **Ride**.
- **History** — your rides ([history.md](history.md)).
- **Profile** — who rides, their figures, editing them and their HUD, new riders
  ([riders.md](riders.md), [hud.md](hud.md)).
- **Devices & Settings** — trainer and heart-rate strap; the ones used last reconnect by
  themselves ([riding.md](riding.md)).

After a ride its summary appears; **Done** returns here.
