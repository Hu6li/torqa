# Ghosts and pacers

Pick someone to race under **Race against** on the course page. The ghost rides the course
as a see-through orange rider next to you, shows as an orange dot on the map and the elevation
profile, and a panel tells you how far behind or ahead you are — in seconds, comparing when
each of you passed your current position.

| Option | The ghost is… |
|---|---|
| Your best on this route | your fastest finished ride on this course (offered once you have one) |
| Pacer at your FTP | a rider holding your FTP all the way |
| Pacer at a power… | a rider holding the power you set |
| Pacer at W/kg… | a rider holding that power per kilogram of *your* weight |
| A recorded activity… | a GPX file with times or a FIT file, e.g. a friend's ride or your outdoor ride |

Pacers have your weight and bike and ride descents like you (coasting or flat), so they climb
and descend realistically — a 250 W pacer is as fast as you would be holding 250 W.

Recorded activities are matched onto the course: the race starts where the activity passed the
course start, and parts that left the course (detours, other roads) are skipped. An activity
that never follows the course is refused with a message.
