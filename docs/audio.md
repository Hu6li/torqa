# Sound

## Ambient sound

Choose **Sound → Ambient sounds** (and a volume) on the setup screen, or *Silent*. During the
ride you hear:

- **wind** that grows louder and brighter with your speed,
- **tyres** on the road, rising in pitch as you go faster,
- **rain** in rainy weather,
- **water** lapping beside lakes and rivers,
- **birds** where the road runs through forest — mostly in the morning, never in the rain and
  rarely in town; wind noise at speed drowns them out.

The surroundings come from OpenStreetMap land cover along the course. All sounds are
synthesised by Torqa; there are no recordings.

## Music

While riding, control the music app you listen to:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| M | Play / pause |
| . | Next track |
| , | Previous track |

- **macOS**: Spotify if it is running, otherwise Apple Music. The first time, macOS asks
  whether Torqa may control the app — allow it (System Settings → Privacy & Security →
  Automation).
- **Windows**: sends the media keys, which the active player handles.
- **Linux**: any MPRIS player via `playerctl` (install it from your distribution).
